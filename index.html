What is Youth
Entrepreneurship
Challenge?

Youth Entrepreneurship Challenge (YEC) offers an ideal platform for curious young minds to tackle everyday problems with ingenuity and originality. From imparting fundamental concepts of entrepreneurship to refining imaginative ideas, crafting business plans, and presenting to a panel of respected judges — this platform has it all!

Our Vision

Youth Entrepreneurship Challenge aims to ignite the spark of innovation and problem-solving, encouraging young individuals to think outside the box and come up with novel solutions. Through this platform, participants learn to turn their passion into viable ventures and develop essential leadership skills for the future.

FAQs

What is Youth Entrepreneurship Challenge?
YEC is a national-level startup & innovation challenge designed for school students by E-Cell IIT Kanpur.
Who is eligible to participate?
School students from classes 6th to 12th are eligible to participate individually or in teams.
Is there any registration fee?
Registration for YEC is completely free of cost for all school students.
