What is Youth Entrepreneurship Challenge?

Youth Entrepreneurship Challenge (YEC) offers an ideal platform for curious young minds to tackle everyday problems with ingenuity and originality. From imparting fundamental concepts of entrepreneurship to refining imaginative ideas, crafting business plans, and presenting to a panel of respected judges — this platform has it all!

Empowering Tomorrow's Business Leaders

Youth Entrepreneurship Challenge (YEC) aims to ignite the spark of innovation, encouraging school students across India to solve real-world problems with ingenuity, strategy, and entrepreneurial spirit.

1. Ignite Innovation

Inspiring students to think outside the box and turn everyday challenges into creative business opportunities.

2. Build Viable Ventures

Teaching core business strategy, prototyping, business modeling, and pitching to expert startup judges.

3. Future Leadership

Cultivating confidence, public speaking, decision-making, and teamwork skills essential for future pioneers.
